About Spur

Built inside schools. Focused on the work behind them.

For more than a decade, Spur has handled the people and staffing work schools depend on: recruiting, screening, credentialing, onboarding, scheduling, payroll, and support. Now we’re bringing that experience to more of the back office.

Our mission

We run the back office so schools can run the classroom.

Schools are asked to do more with less, while the administrative work keeps growing. We believe the answer is a capable partner who owns the work and delivers the outcome.

That is the company Spur is building: experienced school operators delivering essential services with consistency, responsiveness, and care.
