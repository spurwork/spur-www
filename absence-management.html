Questions worth asking.

How can the software be free?

If you run an active Spur staffing contract, the whole platform is included for the life of that agreement. Staffing is our business, and the software is how districts meet us. If you'd rather just buy the software, that's the Standalone plan.

How does Standalone pricing work?

One dollar per student per year, plus a $500 base, billed annually. It's capped at $19,000, so the biggest districts never pay more, and your price never goes up for the life of your contract.

We're mid-contract with another vendor. Now what?

Standalone is free until your current contract ends, so you can switch on your own timeline without ever paying two vendors at once.

Does it handle time tracking too?

Yes. Spur is absence management and time tracking in one system: timesheets, approvals, and payroll export for hourly staff, alongside the absence and substitute tools.

Do we have to use your substitutes?

No. Your pool gets every job first at your rates. Overflow is opt-in and you set the cutoff. We take nothing from your own subs' pay.

What if you can't cover our area?

We'll tell you before you sign. You still get the software at the same price.

How long is the contract?

One year. No auto-escalator, and your price is locked for the term.

Can you handle period-based high school schedules?

Yes, including partial fills and swaps.
