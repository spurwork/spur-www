---
title: "Which Trainings Do I Have To Complete?"
category: "Qualifications & Trainings"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


Each team may have unique training requirements, which can be either online or in-person. Some trainings are provided directly by Spur, while others are conducted externally. Information regarding required trainings is available on the respective team and job listings.
