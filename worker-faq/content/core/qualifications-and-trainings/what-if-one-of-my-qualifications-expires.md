---
title: "What If One Of My Qualifications Expires?"
category: "Qualifications & Trainings"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


Certain qualifications, such as licenses and certificates, have expiration dates. If a qualification expires, you must provide updated documentation to Spur. Until your qualification is renewed, you will not be eligible to work jobs that require it.
