---
title: "Which Qualifications Are Required To Work Jobs?"
category: "Qualifications & Trainings"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


To work on Spur, you must be legally eligible to work in the United States and meet the legal age of majority in your state. In addition, each team has its own qualification requirements—this may include education, specific trainings, licenses, or professional certificates.
