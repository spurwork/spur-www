---
title: "What Is The Difference Between A Team And A Job On Spur?"
category: "Teams"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


Teams: Groups of workers with similar qualifications who work together. Joining a team is the first step in being eligible for job assignments.

Jobs: Specific assignments posted by workplaces. Once you’re part of a team, you become eligible to pick up jobs posted for that team.
