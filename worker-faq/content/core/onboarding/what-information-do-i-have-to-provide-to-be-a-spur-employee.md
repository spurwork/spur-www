---
title: "What Information Do I Have To Provide To Be A Spur Employee?"
category: "Onboarding"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


As a Spur employee, you must provide:

Basic demographic information.

Tax and direct deposit details.

Documentation of your eligibility to work in the United States.

Consent to a background check for employment purposes.

You are also strongly encouraged to add your work history and a professional photo to enhance your worker profile.
