### Disclosure

Spur, Inc. may request background information about you from a consumer reporting agency for employment purposes. The report may include details about character, reputation, criminal history, credit history, driving records, education, employment, and other background information, in accordance with the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). You have the right to request disclosure of the nature and scope of an investigative consumer report.

### Authorization and release

By providing your consent, you allow Spur to share the report with partners and clients as needed to facilitate placement in employment or contractor roles. Where state law permits, the consent can apply throughout your employment or engagement. By consenting, you also authorize agencies and other sources to furnish requested information.

For the current legal notice, read Spur's [Background Check Disclosure](/background-check-disclosure.pdf). Refer to the FCRA materials and consumer-rights summary provided during onboarding for your complete rights.
