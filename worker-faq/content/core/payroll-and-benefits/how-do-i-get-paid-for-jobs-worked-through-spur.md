---
title: "How Do I Get Paid For Jobs Worked Through Spur?"
category: "Payroll And Benefits"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


Spur employees are paid via direct deposit on a weekly basis. The pay period runs from Monday midnight to Sunday midnight, with payday on Friday. Shifts must be approved and closed to be processed. If a shift is in dispute after the cutoff, payment may be delayed until the next pay period.
