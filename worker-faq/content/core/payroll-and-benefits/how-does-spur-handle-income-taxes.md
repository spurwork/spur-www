---
title: "How Does Spur Handle Income Taxes?"
category: "Payroll And Benefits"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


Spur withholds all legally required federal, state, and local taxes based on your residence and the location of the job. If you work in multiple states, taxes are withheld according to the state where the job was performed. A Form W-2 will be provided by January 31 for work completed in the previous year.
