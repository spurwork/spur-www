Alice is Spur’s partner for offering pre-tax savings benefits, including Health Savings Accounts (HSAs), Flexible Spending Accounts (FSAs), pre-tax commuter benefits, and more. Alice helps you save on everyday expenses, potentially increasing your take-home pay. Connect your card to Alice, and if an opportunity arises to boost your paycheck, Alice will identify it for you.
