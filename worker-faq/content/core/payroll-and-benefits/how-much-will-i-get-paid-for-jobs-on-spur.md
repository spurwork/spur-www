---
title: "How Much Will I Get Paid For Jobs On Spur?"
category: "Payroll And Benefits"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


Workplaces set the pay rate for Spur jobs. Your gross pay is calculated by multiplying the hourly rate by the time worked. Spur will withhold taxes and any applicable wage garnishments. Overtime (over 40 hours in a pay period) is paid at 1.5 times the regular rate.
