---
title: "Can I Request Family Or Medical Leave?"
category: "Payroll And Benefits"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


Eligible employees can request up to 12 weeks (or 26 weeks for Injured Service Member Family Leave) of unpaid Family and Medical Leave (FMLA). To be eligible, you must have:

Worked for Spur for at least 12 months.

Completed at least 1,250 hours in the previous 12 months.

Worked at a location meeting the employee count criteria.

Provided proper notice and required certification.

Refer to the FMLA guidelines or contact Spur for more details.
