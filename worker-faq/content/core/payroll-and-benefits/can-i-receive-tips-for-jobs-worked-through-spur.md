---
title: "Can I Receive Tips For Jobs Worked Through Spur?"
category: "Payroll And Benefits"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


Some Spur jobs allow workers to receive tips—this is determined by the workplace. Tips are handled externally (typically “cashed out” at the end of the shift) and must be reported for tax purposes if received outside of the app.
