---
title: "How Do I View My Jobs?"
category: "Jobs"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


Log in to your Spur app.

Upcoming Jobs: Tap the calendar icon at the bottom of the app to view your upcoming jobs.

Past Jobs: Tap on the day you wish to review. For past months, scroll left or right to navigate to the correct month and then select the day.

Job Details & Cancellation: Tap on a specific job to view its details. To cancel a job, tap the three dots next to the job summary, then provide a reason and explanation.
