---
title: "How Do I Clock In And Out?"
category: "Jobs"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


You’ll use Spur to clock in when you begin your shifts and clock out when your shifts end. You do this from within the app. Be sure to clock out for break times and at the end of your shift to help avoid time disputes and to ensure your payment is processed quickly.

**Steps**

Log in to your Spur app.

Click on the calendar icon at the bottom of the screen.

Tap on “Start Shift” to begin your shift.

Tap “Start Break” when beginning your break, and then tap “End Break” when your break is over.

Tap “End Shift” when your shift is completed.
