---
title: "Where Can I See Information About My Jobs?"
category: "Jobs"
source: "Spur Staffing Help Center FAQ"
priority: core
---


Your Timeline in the Spur app is the central location for viewing information about your jobs. From your Timeline, you can:

See details for both upcoming and past jobs.

Manage and adjust your time entries.

Review workplace information after a job ends.

Cancel a job if necessary.

Access your Timeline by clicking the calendar icon at the bottom of the app.
