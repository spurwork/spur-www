### Assess the Injury

If the injury is severe or life-threatening, call 911 immediately.

If the injury is less severe, provide first aid if possible.

### Notify Spur

Notify Spur immediately about the injury if you’re able to. If you cannot notify, a coworker or nearby party should do so.

You can notify Spur by calling (256) 367-8402.

### Obtain Required Forms

Spur Medical Release Form: Once you notify Spur, you’ll receive a medical release form to take with you to an approved medical provider.

First Report of Injury Form: You will also receive a First Report of Injury Form. It is crucial to complete and return this form within 48 hours (meeting the 5-business-day reporting deadline) regardless of whether you seek medical attention. Failure to do so may affect claim eligibility.

### Seek Medical Attention

For severe injuries, report immediately to the closest approved Spur Occupational Health Group provider (a list of approved providers will be provided when you report your injury).
