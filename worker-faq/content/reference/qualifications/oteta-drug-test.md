Omnibus Transportation Employee Testing Act Drug Test with Orange County Public Schools

The mandatory drug test must be taken no more than 30 days prior to the start of orientation.

Drug/OTETA testing is conducted on **Mondays** and **Wednesdays** at** 8:00 a.m**. Please remember that OCPS has to reserve placement for the appointment no later than noon the previous business day. **Please contact Spur Support at 866-957-9142 to notify OCPS when you would like to schedule your screening.** If either testing day falls on a holiday, we revert to an adjusted schedule.

Report to **445 W Amelia St, Orlando Florida 32801**

Park in the ground lot across the side street to the East. Parking is very difficult at this location. Please allow for extra time.

The next set of instructions are crucial to avoid confusion and being incorrectly processed:

1.  Enter the main entrance on Amelia St. then turn right at the main information desk (don’t stop).

2.  Enter the main HR/Employment Services office immediately on the right.

3.  Let the receptionist know that they are there to see “The Employment Coordinator for Transportation/Area West for OTETA testing as a SPUR vendor.”  If asked, they are not there for PREO.

Testing results can take up to two weeks to process. Once verification is received from OCPS please upload the document into the OTETA Drug Test qualification in the Spur app.
