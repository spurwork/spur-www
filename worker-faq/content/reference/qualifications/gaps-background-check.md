To receive a GAPS Background Check, follow the instructions below.

Obtaining this Qualification

1.  From [Gemalto’s website](https://pci.aps.gemalto.com/gaperlpub/registration_ga_pci.pl?UserID=&UserType=&i_ori=&agency_group=EA&agency_subgroup=PU) (also linked below), read and accept the terms and conditions.

2.  On the application form, selecting the following:

- _Agency_: Meriwether County Board of Education

- _Reason_: School Employment – Public Schools

3.  Complete the rest of the application form, and select *Continue*.

4.  Print the confirmation page, which should include your registration ID (REG ID).

5.  Return to [Gemalto’s website](https://www.aps.gemalto.com/ga/index.htm) to find a fingerprint location near you. Follow the instructions for the location near you to schedule your fingerprinting appointment.

6.  Take your confirmation page with you when you complete your fingerprints.

Documenting this Qualification

Upload a copy of your confirmation page in the Spur app.

For more information, visit [https://pci.aps.gemalto.com/gaperlpub/registration_ga_pci.pl?UserID=&UserType=&i_ori=&agency_group=EA&agency_subgroup=PU](https://pci.aps.gemalto.com/gaperlpub/registration_ga_pci.pl?UserID=&UserType=&i_ori=&agency_group=EA&agency_subgroup=PU)

For information on fingerprinting locations, visit [https://www.aps.gemalto.com/ga/index.htm](https://www.aps.gemalto.com/ga/index.htm)
