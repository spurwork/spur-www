To receive a Fulton County, Georgia Background Check, follow the instructions below.

**NOTE:** In order to work in Fulton County, Georgia, you must complete a state background check.

Obtaining this Qualification

1.  Register and pay for the fingerprint background check at [https://www.aps.gemalto.com/indext.htm](https://www.aps.gemalto.com/index.htm)

1.  Click on **Georgia**

2.  Click on **Applicant Registration**

3.  Click on **Education Agencies (EA)**

4.  Click on **Public Schools**

5.  Read Privacy Rights; checkbox (“I have read and accepted these terms”); click **Continue**

6.  For “Agency”, select **Fulton County Board of Education** from the drop-down list

7.  For “reason”, select **School Employment – Public Schools or Volunteer Providing direct care** from the drop-down list

8.  For “Position Applied for”, enter **Company Name and School/Department Name**

9.  For “Fingerprint Card User”, skip and DO NOT check the box

10. For “Payment”, select **Credit Card or Money Order** from the drop-down list (**\$49.25 total cost**)

11. Complete all areas highlighted in yellow. Click **Continue** and follow the instructions on the screen

2.  Proceed to be fingerprinted at an approved location (sites and hours can be found at [https://www.aps.gemalto.com/index.htm](https://www.aps.gemalto.com/index.htm) , then select **Find a Fingerprint Location** from the left side of the page)

3.  Report to the Fulton County Schools Administrative Building located at 6201 Powers Ferry Road, Atlanta, GA 30339 to be photographed and badged (no earlier than 2 days, but no later than 30 days after being fingerprinted). Photographing and badging occurs on Tuesday, Thursday and Friday (9:00 am – 11:00 am and 2:00 pm – 3:00 pm) Contact the Department of Safety & Security at 470-254-7159 with questions or comments.

Documenting this Qualification

Upload a copy of your Fulton County Schools Spur Contractor badge in the Spur app.
