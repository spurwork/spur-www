---

title: Sub License Submission Instructions by District (AL)

category: Qualifications

---

Albertville City Schools

Take your completed substitute license application, proof of payment (either the money order or the printed receipt from paying online), and copy of your high school diploma (or higher) to the Albertville City Schools Board of Education at 107 West Main Street (Albertville, AL 35950).

Montgomery Public Schools

Take your completed substitute license application, and proof of payment (either the money order or the printed receipt from paying online) to Montgomery Public Schools Board of Education at 307 S. Decatur Street (Montgomery, AL 36104).

NOTE: You must take your information to the Board of Education on a Wednesday, between 8 a.m. and 3 p.m.

Russellville City Schools

Mail your completed substitute license application, proof of payment (either the money order or the printed receipt from paying online), and copy of your high school diploma (or higher) to the Russellville City Board of Education at 1945 Waterloo Road (Russellvile, AL 35653).

Selma City Schools

Take your completed substitute license application, proof of payment (either the money order or the printed receipt from paying online), and copy of your high school diploma (or higher) to the Selma City Board of Education at 2194 Broad Street (Selma, AL 36701).
