---

title: Highly Qualified Paraprofessional (GA)

category: Qualifications

---

In order to work for this team, you must have one of the following qualifications:

- Successful completion of at least two years (60 credit hours) of study at an institution of higher education, or

- An Associate’s Degree from an accredited institution of higher education, or

- Successful completion of the GACE Paraprofessional Assessment (with a score of 250 or higher).

Associate’s Degree

Upload or take a photo of your official transcripts, clearly showing successful completion of an associate’s degree from an accredited institution.

College Coursework (60+ Hours)

To document this qualification, upload or take a photo of your official transcripts, clearly showing successful completion of at least 60 hours of coursework.

GACA Paraprofessional Assessment

Complete and pass the GACE Paraprofessional Assessment. You must obtain a score of 250 or higher. Click the “Registration” tab on the GACE Paraprofessional Assessment website for information on testing locations.

Once you complete the assessment, document this qualification by uploading a copy or taking a photo of your results.
