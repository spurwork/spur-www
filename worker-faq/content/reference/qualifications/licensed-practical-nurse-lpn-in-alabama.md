---

title: Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) in Alabama

category: Qualifications

---

Licensed Practical Nurses must be licensed through the Alabama Board of Nursing and first graduate from an accredited nursing program. If you have your nursing license in another state, you are eligible for licensure by endorsement in Alabama.

Documenting this Qualification

- OPTION 1: Upload a copy of your nursing license, or take a photo of the license.

- OPTION 2: Navigate to the [Alabama Board of Nursing License lookup](https://abn.alabama.gov/applications/LicenseLookup.aspx)

- Search for your license using the lookup fields on the page. When your certificate appears, click the “Print” button on the page, and set your printer to “Save as PDF”. Download the PDF of your results, and upload it as documentation.
