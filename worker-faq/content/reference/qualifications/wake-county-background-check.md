---

title: Wake County Background Check

category: Qualifications

---

Wake County will verify you are able to work within the school district using their background check process. Once verified you join the Trainee: Child Nutrition Assistant team to receive the next date and time for in-person Wake Co. orientation.

Obtaining this Qualification

1.  Join the Wake Co. Child Nutrition Assistant Interest Team on the app.

2.  Please check your personal email for the secure Wake Co. Background Check Verification to complete. The subject line will read “Employment Criminal Background Search Request”.

3.  HR Department of Wake Co. will verify your background check.
