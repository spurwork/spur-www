title: Alabama 5-Panel Drug Test

category: Qualifications

---

To receive an Alabama 5-Panel Drug Test, follow the instructions below.

Obtaining this Qualification

1.  Email [support@spurstaffing.com](mailto:support@spurstaffing.com) requesting the form for application with AFC regarding your 5-Panel Drug Test.

2.  Once the request is processed, you will receive a form to complete and send back to our processing agent at Spur.

3.  Complete the 5-Panel Drug Test after scheduling your appointment.

4.  Upload a copy or take a photo of the completed form provided by AFC.

Documenting this Qualification

Once your test results are cleared through AFC, we will approve your 5-Panel Drug Test results in Spur.
