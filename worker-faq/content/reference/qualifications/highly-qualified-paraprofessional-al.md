---

title: Highly Qualified Paraprofessional (AL)

category: Qualifications

---

In order to work for this team, you must have one of the following qualifications:

- Successful completion of at least two years (48 credit hours) of study at an institution of higher education, or

- An Associate’s Degree from an accredited institution of higher education, or

- Successful completion of the Applied Math, Workplace Documents, and Business Writing tests of the ACT WorkKeys Assessment (with a score of 3 or higher on each).

Associate’s Degree

- **OPTION 1: **Upload or take a photo or your associate’s degree diploma.

- **OPTION 2: **Upload or take a photo of your official transcripts, clearly showing successful completion of an associate’s degree from an accredited institution.

College Coursework (48+ Hours)

To document this qualification, upload or take a photo of your official transcripts, clearly showing successful completion of at least 48 hours of coursework.

ACT WorkKeys Assessment

Complete and pass the following three ACT WorkKeys assessments:

- Applied Math,

- Workplace Documents

- Business Writing

You must obtain a score of 3 or higher on each assessment.

To schedule your assessments online. [Click here for locations.](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/workkeys-for-educators/shared/test-site-locator-form.html)

Once you complete the assessments, document this qualification by uploading a copy or taking a photo of your results. Be sure to take a photo of all the assessments in one photo.
