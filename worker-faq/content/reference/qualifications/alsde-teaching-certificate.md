To receive a ALSDE Teaching Certificate, follow the instructions below.

**NOTE:** DO NOT contact ALSDE or the School district directly if you have questions. Please read the information below, and contact Spur if you have additional questions.

Obtaining this Qualification

The [Alabama State Department of Education](https://www.alsde.edu/ofc/otl/Pages/home.aspx) issues professional teaching certificates to candidates who have successfully completed the requirements of a teacher education program from an approved, Alabama educator preparation program. More information can be found on the Alabama State Department of Education website.

Documenting this Qualification

- OPTION 1: Upload a copy of your valid teaching certificate from the Alabama State Department of Education in the Spur app.

- OPTION 2: Navigate to the [ALSDE Certification Search](https://tcert.alsde.edu/Portal/Public/Pages/SearchCerts.aspx)

- Search for your background check results using the lookup fields on the page. When your certificate appears, click the “Print” button in the upper, left corner of the page, and set your printer to “Save as PDF”. Download the PDF of your results, and upload it as documentation. Or, when your certificate appears, take a photo of it, and upload that photo as documentation.

For more information, visit [https://www.alsde.edu/ofc/otl/Pages/home.aspx](https://www.alsde.edu/ofc/otl/Pages/home.aspx)
