Welcome to Spur!

Our vision is to be the most worker-centric company. What does this mean for you? It means reliable work opportunities at your current workplace and at other workplaces, access to better quality healthcare, faster pay, training opportunities, and so much more!

There are a few things you will need to complete the worker onboarding process.

You will need:

*   Smartphone with access to the internet

*   Government issued identification documents like your passport or driver’s license/social security card

*   Someone that’s not related to you to verify your identification documents

*   Bank routing number and bank account number – you can find both of these on a check

Once you have the items above, please see the attachment below on the steps to complete the process.
