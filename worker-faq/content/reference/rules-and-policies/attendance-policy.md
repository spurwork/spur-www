---

title: Attendance Policy

category: Rules and Policies

---

Having good attendance is critical to maintaining a good reputation as a reliable worker. The better your attendance, the easier it will be for you to get the most desirable and best-paying jobs. Below are our attendance requirements:

- Good Standing: Less than 5% of shifts missed

- Probation: 5% – 10% of shifts missed

- Grounds for Termination: More than 10% of shifts missed, no-showing a shift, or canceling your first shift

Cancellation of your first shift on Spur is grounds for immediate suspension or termination.
