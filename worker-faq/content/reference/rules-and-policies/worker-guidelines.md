---

title: Worker Guidelines

category: Rules and Policies

---

Show workplaces that you’re excited to be at work! Here are a few guidelines to follow if you are worker for Spur.

Do:

- Arrive a few minutes early.

- Follow each workplace’s dress code policy.

- Clock in and out at the right time for every shift.

- Respect your colleagues and customers at each workplace.

- Perform each job to the best of your ability.

- Promptly communicate issues to your workplace supervisor.

Don’t

- Fail to show up for a job or repeatedly cancel jobs. This could get you removed from Spur.

- Show up late or take excessive breaks.

- Be on your phone while working.

- Act unprofessionally at work.

- Show up late or under the influence of alcohol/drugs.
